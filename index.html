FAQ

Before you book.

Eight questions founders ask before the first call.

What does a typical engagement look like?
A kickoff call to scope the work, a one-page proposal with deliverables and dates, then weekly builds with a Friday demo. Most engagements run four to twelve weeks. You see working software every week, not a status deck.
What's your minimum engagement length?
Two weeks. Shorter than that and the setup cost outweighs the value. For sub-two-week fixes I'll usually point you to someone better suited.
Do you work with non-technical founders?
Yes — most of my clients are. I translate technical trade-offs into founder language and ask for decisions, not specifications. If you can describe the user and the outcome, I can scope the build.
What stack do you build on?
Web: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind. Mobile: React Native with Expo. Backend: PHP and Laravel, sometimes Node.js. Database: PostgreSQL or MySQL with Redis for caching. I'll pick the boring, proven option unless you have a reason not to.
Can you take over an existing codebase?
Yes. I'll do a paid two-day audit first — read the code, map the debt, and tell you honestly whether to keep building on it or start fresh. Either way you keep the audit.
How do you handle code ownership?
You own it. Repos live in your GitHub from day one. On handoff you get the code, the deploy pipeline, the env documentation, and a Loom walkthrough of anything non-obvious. No lock-in, ever.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Yes, as a monthly retainer with a defined response window. Most founders keep me on for the first three months post-launch, then hand off to an in-house hire once they hire one.
What's your availability right now?
I usually take one or two engagements at a time. The availability pill at the top of the page is live — if it says "available", I'm booking start dates within the next 2 weeks.
